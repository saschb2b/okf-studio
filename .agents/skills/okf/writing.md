# Writing OKF concepts

Use this resource only when a capability produces or revises human-facing concept prose. The OKF specification still decides conformance. These rules decide whether the prose helps its intended reader.

## Establish the reader job

Name the reader and the question or decision the concept supports. Open with the answer or governing fact. Do not announce that an explanation follows.

Match the bundle's language and established register. Keep exact terminology from standards, APIs, legal text, formulas, code, and attributed quotations. When the bundle has no clear house style, use plain professional prose with concrete subjects and verbs.

## Preserve knowledge

Inventory claims before drafting or revision. Keep each fact, number, qualifier, uncertainty marker, citation, link, formula, code sample, and domain term unless the accepted task explicitly changes it. Distinguish sourced facts, user decisions, inferences, and unknowns.

A style-only revision cannot add or remove a factual claim. Route either change through enrichment and attach evidence. A shorter result that loses knowledge is wrong.

## Choose the information shape

Use prose for reasoning and causal explanation. Use a table for field-by-field facts, a definition list for terms, a task list for stateful steps, a Mermaid diagram for topology, and TeX for formal definitions. Do not turn connected reasoning into a decorative list. Do not add a heading or bold label merely to make a short concept look substantial.

## Edit the draft

- Start at the point. Remove throat-clearing and chatbot narration.
- Prefer a named subject and active verb over abstract claims of value.
- Replace vague authority with a named source or remove the claim.
- Remove repeated conclusions, summary echoes, inflated significance, and generic closing claims.
- Use transitions only when they carry logic.
- Vary sentence length without clipping the prose into fragments.
- Avoid em dashes, decorative tricolons, and the inflated "not just X, but Y" cadence.

Phrase matches are clues, not bans. A quotation or established term stays exact. Technical density is acceptable when the reader job requires it.

## Reconcile the result

Compare the final draft with the claim inventory. Classify every claim as unchanged, reworded, added, or removed. Confirm that citations and links still support the same claims and targets. Surface any semantic change instead of hiding it as editing.

## Examples

Weak:

> This powerful feature provides a robust and seamless way to manage knowledge. It is important to note that it also improves collaboration.

Useful:

> Reviewed staging keeps agent edits outside the bundle until the user validates and applies them. This closes the gap between agent-generated prose and an accepted knowledge change.

Wrong revision:

> Refunds are excluded from revenue.

The source says refunds are excluded only after settlement. Removing that qualifier changes the metric and fails the preservation check.

Correct revision:

> Revenue excludes refunds after settlement.
